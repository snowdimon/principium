## Interdisciplinary Context: Principium

### Cybernetics & Systems Theory
Classical goal-setting is oriented toward achievable results. But how do you set a goal that lies beyond the observer's life and still maintain feedback? Principium offers the «Cathedral» model — a goal scaled beyond a biological term, turning the absence of a finale from a tragedy into an architectural advantage.

### Philosophy
The problem of finitude and meaning in the face of death (Heidegger, Existentialism). Principium translates the existential fear of «I will not see the result» into constructive action: «I am laying a foundation that others will see». This is not escapism, but an acceptance of one's role in a long chain of creation.

### Psychology
Fear of uncertainty and the need for immediate reward lead to the avoidance of long-term projects. Principium legitimizes the absence of quick results and reduces anxiety by replacing «I must finish» with «I must begin».
