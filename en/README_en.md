# Principium — The Beginning

[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-CC%20BY--SA%204.0-blue)](LICENSE)
[![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-active-brightgreen)]()

**Maxim:**
*Non sedebis sub tecto quod aedificas. Incipe tamen.*
You will never sit beneath the roof you are building. And yet — begin.

---

### 1. Diagnosis

Modern life operates within a short horizon. Two years, five years, perhaps ten. Beyond that — fog. Society rewards speed: MVPs, quarterly reports, growth by the next round. In this environment, thinking thirty, fifty, or a hundred years ahead appears not just alien, but meaningless.

Those who decide to enter this fog face two barriers:

- **The barrier of solitude.** Society will not understand. Friends and family will ask: «When will there be a result?» Investors, markets, the public — all are tuned to short cycles. The Architect of the long-term is left alone with their design, and this solitude can extinguish any flame.
- **The barrier of invisibility.** In a short horizon, results are visible. In a long one, decades are spent on invisible work: designing foundations, refining principles, cultivating carriers. The outside world sees no progress. The Architect feels as though they are standing still.

If these barriers are not overcome at the very start, they will stop anyone. Therefore, Principium is not about *how* to build. It is about *how to dare to build*.

---

### 2. Axiomatics

The Architect's first step is internal. Before designing a system, one must design one's relationship with time, with the finitude of one's own life, and with one's own ego. Without this, any long-term endeavor will turn either into burnout or into self-deception.

---

### 3. The Protocols of Principium

#### 3.1. Choosing the Cathedral
The Architect defines for themselves a task whose scale exceeds their biological term. This can be a system, a principle, an idea, a semantic framework.

Criteria:
- The task cannot be completed by a single person within a single lifetime.
- Its value does not vanish if the creator disappears.
- It requires transmission: to students, to documentation, to culture.

Without choosing a Cathedral, thinking beyond a lifetime remains an abstraction. With this choice, it becomes an engineering task.

#### 3.2. Humility Before Time and Incompleteness
The Architect accepts as fact: they will not see the completion and will not say everything. This is not a defeat, but a condition of the task.

Hence:
- A shift of focus from «I built» to «I laid the foundation».
- Documenting not only decisions, but also open questions.
- Transmitting not a dogma, but a direction.

#### 3.3. Laying the First Stone
The choice is made. Humility is accepted. Now action is required. Not grandiose. Small. But real.

The first stone is:
- One page of text describing the design.
- One published principle.
- One conversation with a potential carrier.
- One file in a repository.

The point is not the scale, but the transition from intention to embodiment. Before the first stone, the Architect is a dreamer. After — a builder.

---

### 4. Readiness Metrics

| Metric | Code | Question to ask oneself | What to watch for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cathedral Selection | CS | Has a task exceeding my lifespan been defined? | If not, everything else lacks a foundation |
| First Stone | FS | Has it been laid? Is there an action that has translated the design into reality? | If not, there is a risk of remaining a dreamer |
| Openness of the Finale | OP | Have my questions been documented, not just my answers? | If not, I leave a dogma, not a foundation |

---

### 5. Applicability

Principium is the zero step for anyone who claims to create systems, ideas, or meanings with a horizon that exceeds a human life. It does not require special knowledge or status. It requires only honesty with oneself and a readiness to begin.

Principium precedes all the other principles of the Doctrine. **Anti-Icarus** teaches how not to burn out on the journey. **Absurdum ad Veritatem** — how to test what has been built. But none of them have meaning unless the first act has been performed: the decision to build, knowing that you will never sit beneath the roof.
